# Tbwiqa

**Tbwiqa** (Moroccan Arabic: **تبويقة**; `/tbwiːqɑ/`) is a Moroccan slang word used as a noun for **intoxicants** and as a verb meaning **to get high**. It is related to the adjective **mbwaq** (**مبوق**; `/mbwɑːq/`), meaning "high," and comes from the word **bowaqa** (**بواقة**; `/buwɑːqɑ/`), which means "bubbles." Comparing a buzz to rising bubbles is the exact same visual idea behind **squeans**, the floating bubbles drawn around a character's head in comics and anime to show they are dizzy or drunk.

# Tbwiqa: The Game

**Tbwiqa** is an arcade "brick-less" Brick Breaker game with the goal of hitting randomly spawned target corresponding to *-(currently)-* 4 dr\*gs; Alcohol, Weed, LSD and Whippet.
